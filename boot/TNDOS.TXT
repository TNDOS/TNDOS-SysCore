TNDDOS -- a DOS redefined for the UEFI era.

This file exists so that external commands have something to chew on.
FIND searches it, MORE pages it, FC can diff it against a copy.

The point of TNDDOS is not to move DOS onto UEFI.
It is to keep DOS's interaction model and drop its memory model.

So: fixed segments, one program at a time, no protection -- on purpose.
But no 640K barrier, no himem, no XMS, no EMM386, no conventional memory.

TNX is the executable format. A PE loader is eight hundred lines of
edge cases; TNX is a memcpy and a jump. That is the whole argument.
